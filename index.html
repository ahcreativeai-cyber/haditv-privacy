Hadi TV app: privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

The Hadi TV mobile app lets you watch Hadi TV's live channels. We respect your privacy.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no sign-up or account, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Streaming

To play a channel, your device connects to Hadi TV's streaming servers. As with any website or video service, those servers receive your device's IP address while the video is delivered. It is not used to identify you.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
